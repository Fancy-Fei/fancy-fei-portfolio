ABOUT ME

I’m Fancy Fei, a graphic designer interested in how design can make ideas tangible and invite people to look closer.

My work spans visual identity, editorial design, print and moving image. I often begin with an observation or question, then explore it through research, material experiments and visual storytelling.

I’m interested in refining every visual decision so that colour, texture, typography and interaction each serve a clear purpose. For me, design is a thoughtful, logical response to an idea or question—where every choice has a reason.
